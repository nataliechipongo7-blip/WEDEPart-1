About Our Bakery

Candice Cakes is a small home-based bakery dedicated to making beautiful handmade cakes for every celebration.Unlike a large factory or a commercial cake shop,every single cake is baked right at home using traditional methods and fresh ingredients.This home based approach allows for a level of care and attention that mass-produced cakes simply cannot match. From rich chocolate layers to light and fluffy vanilla sponges,each cake is crafted with love and precision to ensure the highest quality and delicious taste. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, or any special occasion, Candice Cakes is committed to creating unforgettable moments with our delectable creations.

Our Vision

To bake affordable, high-quality cakes from home that bring a personal and delicious touch to every celebration in the community.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional handmade cakes that exceed customer expectations and create memorable experiences for every celebration.

Our Values

  • Fresh ingredients
  • Creative cake designs
  • Friendly customer service
